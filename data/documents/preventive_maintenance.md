# Preventive Maintenance Scheduling

Document ID: SOP-MAINT-003
Department: Maintenance Engineering
Category: Preventive Maintenance
Version: 1.0

## Purpose

This procedure describes the planning and documentation of preventive
maintenance activities for manufacturing equipment.

## Maintenance Planning

Maintenance activities should be scheduled using approved maintenance
intervals, equipment operating hours, manufacturer guidance, and
documented equipment condition.

## Maintenance Records

A maintenance record should contain:

- Equipment identifier
- Maintenance task identifier
- Scheduled maintenance date
- Actual completion date
- Technician or responsible team
- Parts replaced, if applicable
- Findings and observations
- Outstanding issues

## Overdue Maintenance

Overdue maintenance must be identified and reviewed by the responsible
maintenance team.

Equipment operating decisions must follow the applicable safety,
maintenance, and production authorization procedures.

## Completion

Maintenance is considered documented when the required work,
inspection results, unresolved issues, and responsible personnel are
recorded in the maintenance system.

## Performance Monitoring

Useful maintenance indicators include:

- Preventive maintenance completion rate
- Number of overdue maintenance tasks
- Equipment downtime
- Mean time to repair
- Recurring equipment faults

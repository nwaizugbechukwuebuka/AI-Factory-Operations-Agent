# Manufacturing Quality Defect Management

Document ID: SOP-QUALITY-002
Department: Quality Assurance
Category: Quality Management
Version: 1.0

## Purpose

This procedure describes how production quality defects should be
documented, investigated, and escalated.

## Defect Identification

When a suspected defect is identified, the employee must record the
product identifier, production batch, workstation, timestamp, and
observed defect characteristics.

Where required by the approved quality procedure, the affected product
must be isolated to prevent unintended progression through production.

## Root Cause Investigation

The quality team should review the available evidence, including:

- Product inspection results
- Production timestamps
- Equipment identifiers
- Relevant process parameters
- Previous defect records
- Documented corrective actions

A suspected cause must not be recorded as a confirmed root cause
without supporting evidence.

## Escalation

Repeated defects, defects affecting multiple batches, and defects
presenting potential safety or regulatory concerns must be escalated
according to the applicable quality management procedure.

## Corrective Actions

Each corrective action must identify an owner, target date, completion
status, and verification method.

## Closure

A defect record may be closed when the investigation is documented,
required corrective actions are completed or formally tracked, and
the applicable verification requirements are satisfied.
